Qanday ma’lumot yig‘amiz

  • Instagram xabarlari. Siz Instagram’da @taklifim.uz sahifasiga yozgan xabarlar matni va Instagram bergan foydalanuvchi identifikatori (ID).
  • Buyurtma ma’lumotlari. Taklifnoma uchun o‘zingiz yuborgan ma’lumotlar: ismlar, to‘y sanasi va vaqti, to‘yxona manzili, rasmlar va musiqa.
  • Mehmon javoblari. Taklifnoma saytida mehmon kiritgan ism, telefon raqami (ixtiyoriy), kelish-kelmasligi va tilaklari.

Ma’lumotdan qanday foydalanamiz

  • Savollaringizga javob berish, buyurtmani rasmiylashtirish va taklifnomani tayyorlash uchun.
  • Instagram xabarlariga AI yordamchi (Anthropic kompaniyasining Claude xizmati) javob yozadi. Buning uchun suhbat matni shu xizmatga yuboriladi. Sahifa egasi istalgan vaqtda suhbatga o‘zi qo‘shiladi.
  • Mehmon javoblari faqat taklifnoma egalariga (kelin-kuyov) ko‘rsatiladi.

Ma’lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz, reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz va yuqorida aytilganlardan boshqa uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz.

Privacy Policy

Taklifim.uz — online wedding invitations · Last updated: October 8, 2026

Information we collect

  • Instagram messages. The text of messages you send to our Instagram account @taklifim.uz and the Instagram-scoped user ID provided by Instagram.
  • Order details. Information you send us to create an invitation: names, wedding date and time, venue address, photos and music.
  • Guest responses. Name, optional phone number, attendance and wishes entered by guests on an invitation website.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, take your order and create your invitation.
  • Replies to Instagram messages are written by an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic); the conversation text is sent to that service for this purpose. The page owner can join the conversation at any time.
  • Guest responses are shown only to the invitation owners (the couple).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than as described above.

Retention

Data is stored on our server for as long as needed to provide the service and is deleted on request.

Data deletion

To delete your data, send “Delete my data” to @taklifim.uz on Instagram. Your conversation history and order details will be deleted within 7 days and we will confirm it to you.
